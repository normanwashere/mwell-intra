In the Procurement/Finance CSV, an unknown ordered or received quantity is a blank numeric cell with the matching `Ordered Units Availability` or `Received Units Availability` value `unknown`. A verified zero remains `0` with `available`. Do not coerce blanks to zero, sum a partially known total or treat `NaN` as a valid quantity. Existing numeric columns retain their positions; the two availability columns are appended. Check downstream imports by header and retain unknown state. Finance's committed-PO and net-received totals use their displayed period when available.
