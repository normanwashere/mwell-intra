## Scope

The source applies to MPIC procurement of goods and services and related conduct by MPIC employees and officers. Its stated purpose is honest, competitive, fair, sustainable, diverse, and transparent procurement that protects the company, supports sound decisions, considers specification, quality, service, delivery, reliability, environmental, legal, regulatory, social, and life-cycle cost factors, and follows company procedures.

## Direct requirements

The following statements summarize requirements stated directly in the February 2025 MPIC source.

### Intake and solicitation

- Goods requisitions begin with an approved requirement and budget. The source centralizes goods requisitions through MPIC HR Admin and generally prohibits employee cash-advance or reimbursement purchases except in special limited circumstances.
- A **Request for Proposal (RFP)** is the solicitation document for services. It is prepared after budget approval and must give vendors enough information to decide whether to participate and submit a competitive technical and commercial proposal.
- A **Request for Quotation (RFQ)** is the solicitation document for materials. It is prepared after budget approval and includes the material description or specification, vendor identity, terms and instructions, payment and shipping terms, required delivery date, ship-to address, quote validity, reference number, quantity, unit of measure, applicable drawings or other attachments, any pre-qualification requirement, and the bid deadline.
- All invited vendors receive the same requirement and clarifications. Vendors are responsible for valid contact details.
- The source also uses the acronym `RFP` for **Request for Payment** in its acronym list and payment annex. In Mwell documentation, `RFP` means Request for Proposal when discussing solicitation; payment records are written as payment requests to prevent ambiguity.

### Competition and evaluation

- Competitive bidding is the default procurement mode for materials and services.
- Only accredited vendors are invited. The Requestor and MPIC HR Admin select **three to four accredited vendors** for bidding.
- The standard bid window is **at least seven working days**. An extension is limited to **seven calendar days** because the accepted source says days, not working days; all invited vendors are notified, and vendors may revise and resubmit by the revised due date.
- Vendors confirm RFQ receipt within **24 hours** and raise exceptions or clarification points within **48 hours**.
- For sealed bids, at least **three competitive quotations** must be available before opening. Fewer than three, a non-compliant submission mode, all technically non-compliant bids, or bids believed implausibly high or low can result in a failed bid.
- Failed-bid recovery may extend the deadline to source more vendors or allow a re-quote. Proceeding with fewer than three competitive quotations requires a justification; it is not an automatic pass.
- MPIC HR Admin prepares the bid tabulation within **48 hours** after bid receipt. When technical evaluation is needed, the Requestor returns comments within **five working days**.
- Best value considers technical compliance, quality or specification, delivery lead time, total cost or cost effectiveness, warranty, after-sales support, price, payment terms, and training cost. Lowest price alone is not an automatic winner.
- When the Requestor recommendation differs from the MPIC HR Admin evaluation, the source requires written justification, Department Head approval, and a Group Controller decision.

### Exceptions and commitments

- Competitive bidding may be waived only for a stated exception, including source-policy petty cash, emergency, fiduciary/confidential or specialist need, unsolicited proposal, repeat purchase, sole or single source, or another specifically approved situation.
- Sole sourcing requires no acceptable open-market alternative and a compatibility, specialization, capability, manufacturer, or authorized-distributor basis.
- A repeat order requires the same price, payment terms, vendor, and other considerations; a prior abstract no more than one year old; an originally competitive transaction; and an amount of PHP 250,000 or below.
- An emergency purchase responds to a threat to life, safety, or the environment, or a serious operational disruption. Verbal commitments are minimized and a PO is documented as soon as possible.
- Source-policy petty cash applies to small purchases of PHP 2,000 or below. This is an exception value, not an approval limit or authority to split purchases.
- Purchases use accredited vendors and a duly approved PO or contract. The PO pack includes the bid tabulation, vendor quote, RFQ or RFP, and approved requisition.
- The source states that vendors acknowledge the PO within **48 hours**. Outstanding POs are reviewed weekly, including partial or late delivery and missing or unposted receiving reports.

### Receiving, vendors, and payment

- Non-conforming deliveries are rejected and replaced. Warranty issues use a merchandise-return notice, and vendors advise MPIC of potential delivery delays.
- Vendors undergo commercial, financial, and technical accreditation. Passing vendors are added to the supplier set and enter a **six-month probation** period.
- The source probation measures are: winning at least 20% of POs, 100% delivery-date compliance, zero delivery returns or rejections, and timely delivery/payment documents.
- Requested test samples are purchased under a PO; sample acceptance is limited to a real requirement with a reasonable sales opportunity.
- Itemized, legible invoices or receipts are primary payment support. Under the source policy, each supplier invoice at PHP 50,000 or more requires a PO; the invoice must reconcile to the approved PO before payment.
- Applicable tax, foreign-supplier, contract, and other supporting evidence accompanies the payment request.

## Mwell mapping

Mwell separates three route axes so a document label cannot silently decide mode or authority.

| Axis | Mwell values | Mapping rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solicitation document | RFQ, RFP, or none | Materials use RFQ; services use RFP; an approved exception may use none unless a policy owner requires a supporting solicitation |
| Procurement mode | Competitive bidding, sole source, repeat order, emergency purchase, petty cash, or another approved exception | Competition is the default; an exception requires eligibility, evidence, owner review, and DOA approval |
| Governance tier | Standard, formal bid, high-risk/special control, and effective DOA route | Amount, complexity, risk, data sensitivity, category, and the current effective DOA determine control depth and authority |

The local Mwell operating policy uses PHP 1,000,000 as a **formal-bid governance** boundary. It does not turn high-value materials into an RFP or low-value services into an RFQ. Examples:

- A high-value material requirement remains an RFQ and follows formal-bid and effective-DOA controls.
- A low-value service requirement remains an RFP and follows the governance tier justified by its facts.
- A sole-source, repeat-order, emergency, or petty-cash request uses an exception mode only after the stated conditions and evidence pass.

Mwell maps MPIC HR Admin operational work to Procurement workflows, technical comments to an assigned technical reviewer, receiving to Warehouse or the service owner, payment validation to Finance, and accreditation decisions to Legal/Vendor Management. These are local workflow responsibilities, not assertions that the named Mwell roles inherit MPIC approval authority.

For recommendation variance, the direct MPIC rule names Department Head approval followed by a Group Controller decision. Current Mwell code maps those stages to `dept_head` and then `finance`. That code mapping is not an authorized Mwell policy decision. Until Mwell Procurement, Finance, department/DOA owners approve the local decision stages and their effective DOA scopes, operating instructions use **first independent variance decision** and **second independent variance decision**, and policy-alignment activation remains blocked.

## Ownership

- **MPIC policy owner:** approves corrections or revisions to the authoritative binary source.
- **Mwell Procurement policy owner:** approves the local solicitation, mode, governance, competition, exception, sourcing, and commitment mapping.
- **Department owner and DOA administrators:** activate effective-dated Mwell approval assignments; no documentation text names an approver.
- **Legal/Vendor Management:** owns accreditation, scoped temporary-clearance, probation, and legal-instrument decisions in the Mwell mapping.
- **Finance:** owns payment evidence, tax/withholding treatment, petty-cash eligibility, and financial authority interpretation.
- **Warehouse/Operations or service owner:** owns receiving, quality disposition, custody, and acceptance evidence within assigned authority.
- **Platform Admin:** administers authorized policy/DOA configuration and access; administration does not create business approval authority.
- **Product and Engineering:** maintain implementation, tests, this extract, and traceability; they do not resolve policy conflicts.
